# RetailIntel data dictionary

All initial observations are reproducible synthetic data generated by this repository.

## Dimensions

### `dim_supplier`
Grain: one supplier.

- `lead_time_days`: contracted/planned replenishment lead time used in supplier and inventory decisions.

### `dim_product`
Grain: one SKU.

- `unit_cost_minor`: reference product cost in integer minor currency units.
- `list_price_minor`: undiscounted synthetic list price.

## Facts

### `fact_order`
Grain: one customer order.

### `fact_order_line`
Grain: one SKU line inside one order.

`quantity` is ordered quantity; `returned_qty` is constrained between zero and ordered quantity. Revenue, returns, COGS, and margin analyses are calculated from this grain.

### `fact_inventory_snapshot`
Grain: calendar day × SKU.

Contains on-hand, on-order, reorder-point, and safety-stock quantities. The latest snapshot drives the first inventory action mart.

### `fact_purchase_order`
Grain: one supplier purchase order for one SKU.

Contains ordered, expected, and received dates so lead-time reliability can be measured rather than assumed.

## Marts

### `mart_product_daily`
Grain: day × SKU.

Decision use: sales after returns, COGS, gross margin, category/SKU performance, and later promotion analysis.

### `mart_inventory_action`
Grain: latest snapshot × SKU.

Decision use: classify stock as `healthy`, `watch`, `reorder`, or `stockout`, while exposing the reorder quantity gap and supplier lead time.

### `mart_supplier_reliability`
Grain: supplier.

Decision use: compare actual lead time, on-time delivery rate, late days, and purchase-order volume before making replenishment or supplier decisions.
